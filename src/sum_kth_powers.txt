The first seven examples of Faulhaber's formula are:

* Sum of k^0 = n
* Sum of k^1 = 1/2 * (n^2 + n)
* Sum of k^2 = 1/3 * (n^3 + 3/2*n^2 + 1/2*n)
* Sum of k^3 = 1/4 * (n^4 + 2*n^3 + n^2)
* Sum of k^4 = 1/5 * (n^5 + 5/2*n^4 + 5/3*n^3 - 1/6*n)
* Sum of k^5 = 1/6 * (n^6 + 3*n^5 + 5/2*n^4 - 1/2*n^2)
* Sum of k^6 = 1/7 * (n^7 + 7/2*n^6 + 7/2*n^5 - 7/6*n^3 + 1/6*n)
